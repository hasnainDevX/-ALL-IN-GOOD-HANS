// Primary Colors
Cream Beige: #ECDAC8
- Use for: Main backgrounds, light sections, card backgrounds

Soft Peach: #D1A693  
- Use for: Secondary backgrounds, hover states, subtle accents

Terracotta: #D8966F
- Use for: Medium accents, borders, decorative elements

Rust: #BF5B3C
- Use for: Primary CTAs, headings, important text

Deep Brown: #955749
- Use for: Text, icons, footer, strong emphasis

// Color Hierarchy
Background: #ECDAC8 (Cream Beige)
Headings: #BF5B3C (Rust) or #955749 (Deep Brown)
Body Text: #955749 (Deep Brown) or slightly darker
Accents: #D8966F (Terracotta)
Hover/Interactive: #D1A693 (Soft Peach)
CTAs: #BF5B3C (Rust) with #955749 (Deep Brown) on hover
